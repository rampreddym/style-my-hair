# Mirra Challenge Film — Natural Story and Real AI Demo

## Goal
Replace the current film with a polished 2–3 minute, 1920×1080 product story that sounds human and proves Mirra’s core journey through straight-on, real app footage.

## Story
1. Open with the familiar disappointment of leaving a salon with a result that did not match the idea in your head.
2. Introduce Mirra as a way to explore the look at home before making the appointment.
3. Follow one fictional customer through five-angle photo upload, entering “bob cut with dark brown hair,” generating a preview, and dragging the before/after comparison.
4. Show how the chosen image and practical instructions become a visual brief the stylist can review before the visit.
5. Continue through stylist discovery, service and time selection, Stripe card payment, appointment confirmation, and the stylist’s view of the booking.
6. Close with a grounded question about how much easier a salon visit becomes when customer and stylist begin with the same picture.

- Keep the existing problem → solution → reflective closing arc and retain only defensible research statistics with a clear source note.
- Generate a consistent fictional adult model in five head angles. Do not use scraped portraits or the owner’s personal photos.
- Create a temporary demo customer, remove personal images from the demo journey, upload the five synthetic portraits, and record the actual app interactions.
- Capture the exact AI prompt, generation result, favorite selection, and touch-driven before/after slider. If the live AI service blocks the request, show the safe error honestly rather than fabricating a successful interaction.
- Record the shareable stylist brief and payment flow where the current product supports them; describe Stripe as the card-payment integration without implying unverified settlement behavior.
- Keep every phone and app view perfectly upright: zero rotation, skew, or perspective tilt.
- Retain the warm editorial chocolate-and-cream visual language, restrained typography, narration-led mix, and upbeat background track.
