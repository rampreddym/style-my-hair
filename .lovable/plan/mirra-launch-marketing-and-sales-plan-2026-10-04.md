# Mirra Launch, Marketing and Sales Plan

## Decisions
- Market: one U.S. city (city to be confirmed; I recommend picking where you can personally meet stylists)
- Budget: $0, organic only
- Launch: in about 30 days
- Deliverable: full strategy document + 30 days of ready-to-post content for every platform

## Approach
Mirra needs stylists before customers. The plan runs in three phases:

```text
Days 1-10   Sign up 15-25 local stylists (walk-ins, DMs, salon groups)
Days 11-24  Build a customer waitlist (teasers, AI try-on posts, referrals)
Days 25-30  Launch week, then weekly review of what worked
```

## What I will research first
- Search what people in the U.S. look for ("haircut preview", "try hairstyles on my face", "stylist near me")
- Check how Booksy, StyleSeat, Fresha and AI hairstyle apps market themselves on social
- Find active local Facebook groups, subreddits and creators for the chosen city, with posting rules
- Saved as a competitor and community research file for reuse

## The strategy document will cover
1. Positioning: "The Uber for your hairstyling" and "See it before you cut it"
2. Who it is for: customer types and stylist types, their main frustrations
3. Stylist sales plan: pitch script, walk-in flyer text, DM templates, objection answers, onboarding checklist
4. Customer growth: waitlist, referral rewards, before/after challenge, local creator swaps (free styling for posts)
5. Platform playbook: Instagram, TikTok, Facebook groups, Reddit, X, LinkedIn, Nextdoor, Google Business Profile
6. Launch week schedule, hour by hour on launch day
7. Pricing and revenue model options for stylists (with recommendation, marked for your decision)
8. Weekly numbers to track and what to do if they are low
9. Claims to avoid unless sourced (for example the "75% unhappy" figure)

## 30-day content pack
- Daily posts for Instagram and TikTok (captions, Reel/TikTok scripts, hashtags)
- 3 X posts per week, 1 LinkedIn founder post per week, Facebook group and Reddit posts written to each community's rules
- Stylist recruitment posts and messages
- Spanish versions for the top posts
- Uses the existing product film and real app screens; any image needed beyond that will be listed for you

## Format
- One polished document (PDF) with the strategy, plus a spreadsheet content calendar you can schedule from
- Saved in your Files; the brand voice and launch decisions saved to project memory
