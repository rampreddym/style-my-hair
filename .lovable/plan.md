# Mirra Contra Challenge Video

## Goal
Produce a polished 16:9 product film, approximately 2–3 minutes, presenting Mirra as a mobile-first app for customers and stylists.

## Creative direction
- **Look:** premium warm editorial, matching Mirra’s dark chocolate, cream, and warm taupe interface.
- **Type:** DM Serif Display for expressive statements; Fira Sans for supporting copy and captions.
- **Motion:** smooth phone-camera moves, elegant masked reveals, purposeful push transitions, and restrained kinetic type.
- **Motifs:** floating mobile device, cream editorial rules, small-caps chapter labels, and softly moving salon imagery.
- **Arc:** hook with the fragmented salon journey, reveal Mirra’s unified experience, demonstrate both sides, then finish on the product’s impact and Contra/Lovable build story.

## Production
1. Write a truthful narration script from the implemented app features, targeting 2–3 minutes.
2. Capture mobile customer and stylist journeys from the live app, including authentication, discovery, AI hairstyle preview, booking, appointments, services, availability, profile, portfolio, reviews, and payments where visible.
3. Generate the narration with the supplied ElevenLabs voice and create an original upbeat instrumental bed.
4. Build a 1920×1080 motion-graphics video with an on-screen phone, chapter titles, feature callouts, readable captions, and app footage.
5. Mix narration prominently over music, add subtle transitions, and finish with a strong Mirra end card identifying it as built for the Contra Lovable Challenge.
6. Spot-check key frames, render the final MP4, inspect duration/audio/video metadata, and deliver the finished file.

## Technical details
- Use frame-based Remotion animation and a consistent transition system.
- Render at 30 fps in H.264 with AAC audio.
- Keep app footage muted; narration remains primary and music is ducked beneath it.
- Use the provided ElevenLabs voice ID `sfJopaWaOtauCD3HKX6Q`.
- Preserve only capabilities that exist in the current product; no invented claims.
